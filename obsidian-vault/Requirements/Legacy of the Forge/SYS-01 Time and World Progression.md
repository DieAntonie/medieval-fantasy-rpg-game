**Purpose:** Provide a mechanism for advancing the world independently of the player's immediate actions.

The world must progress as the player works, fulfils commissions, and performs other workshop activities.

#### Core requirements

- **REQ-01.01** The game must maintain a persistent in-game date and time.
    
- **REQ-01.02** The player must be able to advance time through meaningful actions.
    
- **REQ-01.03** Time progression must trigger scheduled customer activities and world events.
    
- **REQ-01.04** The world must not require continuous real-time simulation.
    
- **REQ-01.05** Events and activities must resolve in a predictable sequence when time advances.
    
- **REQ-01.06** The player must receive relevant information about significant events that occurred during the elapsed time.
    

#### Extended requirements

- **REQ-01.07** The world may support different activity schedules depending on the time of day.
    
- **REQ-01.08** The world may include seasonal changes that influence activities, demand, and events.
