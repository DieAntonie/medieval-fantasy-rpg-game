**Purpose:** Populate the world with persistent inhabitants who commission, own, use, and return equipment.

Customers should exist independently of the player and have their own activities and equipment requirements.

#### Core requirements

- **REQ-02.01** Each customer must have a persistent identity.
    
- **REQ-02.02** Each customer must have a profession that influences their activities and equipment requirements.
    
- **REQ-02.03** Customers must be able to own and maintain an inventory of equipment.
    
- **REQ-02.04** Customers must perform activities appropriate to their profession.
    
- **REQ-02.05** Activities must be capable of consuming time and exposing equipment to wear or events.
    
- **REQ-02.06** Customers must be able to commission new equipment and request repairs.
    
- **REQ-02.07** Customers must be able to return to the blacksmith with equipment they own.
    
- **REQ-02.08** Customer histories and relationships must persist between visits.
    

#### Extended requirements

- **REQ-02.09** Customers may acquire, trade, inherit, or lose equipment independently.
    
- **REQ-02.10** Customers may develop relationships that influence commissions, rumours, and interactions.
    
- **REQ-02.11** Customers may have individual characteristics that influence their behaviour and decisions.
