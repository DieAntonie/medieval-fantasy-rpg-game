**Purpose:** Communicate the consequences of the simulated world to the player.

The player should not have perfect knowledge of everything happening outside the forge. Stories, rumours, and returning customers provide an organic way to discover what happens to their creations.

#### Core requirements

- **REQ-09.01** The game must communicate significant world events to the player.
    
- **REQ-09.02** Customers must be able to share information about their equipment and experiences.
    
- **REQ-09.03** The player must be able to discover item histories through customer interactions.
    
- **REQ-09.04** The player must be able to view relevant item information and known history.
    
- **REQ-09.05** Information presented to the player must be based on events and world state.
    
- **REQ-09.06** The game must distinguish between an item's actual history and what the player currently knows about it.
    

#### Extended requirements

- **REQ-09.07** Rumours may be incomplete, exaggerated, or incorrect.
    
- **REQ-09.08** Information may spread through travellers, customers, and settlement locations.
    
- **REQ-09.09** The player may gradually discover the whereabouts of lost or historically significant items.
    
- **REQ-09.10** A workshop ledger may record crafted items, ownership, repairs, and known achievements.
