**Purpose:** Allow the player to manufacture equipment that enters the simulated world.

Smithing is the primary player-facing activity. Crafting should involve meaningful choices that influence the resulting item's properties.

#### Core requirements

- **REQ-03.01** The player must be able to craft weapons, armour, and tools.
    
- **REQ-03.02** Each craftable item must have a defined recipe or set of crafting requirements.
    
- **REQ-03.03** Crafting must consume the required materials.
    
- **REQ-03.04** Crafted items must receive persistent identities.
    
- **REQ-03.05** Each crafted item must record its item type, materials, quality, and initial condition.
    
- **REQ-03.06** Crafting quality must influence relevant item properties.
    
- **REQ-03.07** Completed items must be deliverable to customers or retained by the player.
    
- **REQ-03.08** The player must be able to view the properties of an item before and after crafting.
    

#### Extended requirements

- **REQ-03.09** Different materials may influence durability, weight, appearance, and performance.
    
- **REQ-03.10** Crafting techniques may influence the quality and characteristics of the finished item.
    
- **REQ-03.11** Exceptional craftsmanship may contribute to an item's reputation or future legacy.
    
  
#### UI requirements

- UI-S03.01 The player must be able to access available crafting recipes.
    
- UI-S03.02 The player must be able to inspect the materials and requirements of a recipe.
    
- UI-S03.03 The crafting interface must communicate the expected properties of the resulting item.
    
- UI-S03.04 The player must receive feedback when crafting succeeds or fails.
    
- UI-S03.05 The player must be able to inspect completed items before delivering them.
