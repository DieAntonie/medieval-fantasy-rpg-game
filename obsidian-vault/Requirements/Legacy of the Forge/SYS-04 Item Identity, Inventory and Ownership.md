**Purpose:** Ensure that equipment persists as individual objects throughout the simulation.

An item must be more than a generic equipment type. Two iron spears may have different owners, histories, conditions, and reputations.

#### Core requirements

- **REQ-04.01** Every individual item must have a unique persistent identifier.
    
- **REQ-04.02** Each item must maintain its item type, materials, quality, and durability.
    
- **REQ-04.03** Each item must track its current owner or location.
    
- **REQ-04.04** Ownership changes must be recorded.
    
- **REQ-04.05** Items must retain their identity and history when transferred between owners.
    
- **REQ-04.06** Items must maintain a record of significant events in which they participated.
    
- **REQ-04.07** Items must be able to exist in different states, including owned, stored, displayed, lost, or destroyed.
    

#### Extended requirements

- **REQ-04.08** Items may have individual names assigned by their owners or the player.
    
- **REQ-04.09** Items may develop unique characteristics through their history.
    
- **REQ-04.10** Lost items may be recovered through future events or customer interactions.
