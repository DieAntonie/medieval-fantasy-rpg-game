The systems above must work together rather than operate as isolated mechanics.

- **INT-01** Crafting an item must create a persistent item record.
    
- **INT-02** Delivering an item must associate it with a customer.
    
- **INT-03** Customer activities must be capable of triggering events involving owned equipment.
    
- **INT-04** Events must update equipment condition and history.
    
- **INT-05** Significant events must be capable of changing item legacy.
    
- **INT-06** Returning customers must be able to bring damaged or historically significant equipment back to the forge.
    
- **INT-07** Repairs must preserve item identity and legacy.
    
- **INT-08** Items may eventually be retired and displayed instead of remaining in active use.
    
- **INT-09** Displayed items must be capable of influencing the settlement through their aura.
    
- **INT-10** The player must discover world changes through the information and interaction systems.
