**Purpose:** Allow an item's accumulated legacy to influence the world even when it is no longer actively used.

Aura represents the active influence of an item's reputation, history, and cultural significance.

It is not necessarily a literal magical property. The influence may be psychological, social, cultural, or supernatural depending on the setting.

#### Core requirements

- **REQ-08.01** Aura must be derived from an item's legacy rather than its physical condition.
    
- **REQ-08.02** An item must be capable of retaining its aura when removed from active use.
    
- **REQ-08.03** An item must be capable of being displayed or preserved at a suitable location.
    
- **REQ-08.04** The item's location and display context must influence how its aura affects the world.
    
- **REQ-08.05** Aura effects must be distinguishable from the item's ordinary equipment properties.
    

#### Extended requirements

- **REQ-08.06** Different locations may activate different aspects of an item's aura.
    
- **REQ-08.07** A Hall of Heroes may use an item's history to inspire recruits or improve communal morale.
    
- **REQ-08.08** A tavern may amplify an item's renown through stories and visitors.
    
- **REQ-08.09** A monastery may preserve an item's spiritual or historical significance.
    
- **REQ-08.10** Display capacity, relevance, and cultural saturation may limit the benefits of accumulated relics.
