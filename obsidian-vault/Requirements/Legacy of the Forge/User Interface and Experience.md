**Purpose:** Establish a consistent, understandable and accessible user experience across all gameplay systems.

The game will use a menu-driven interface rather than relying on a continuously simulated 3D environment. The UI must communicate the state of the workshop, the settlement, its inhabitants and their equipment.

### Core requirements

#### Navigation and Layout

- **UI-01** The game must provide a consistent navigation structure through which the player can access its major gameplay systems.
    
- **UI-02** The player must always be able to identify their current location or active interface.
    
- **UI-03** Common information and actions should be presented consistently across different systems.
    
- **UI-04** Navigation should minimise unnecessary transitions between related activities.
    
- **UI-05** The interface must provide a clear way to return to the main workshop view.
    

#### Information Presentation

- **UI-06** Important information must be presented in a clear and understandable format.
    
- **UI-07** Related information should be grouped logically.
    
- **UI-08** The interface must distinguish between actionable information, informational messages and historical records.
    
- **UI-09** Important changes to the world must be communicated to the player.
    
- **UI-10** The interface must provide enough context for the player to understand the consequences of their actions.
    

#### Feedback and Interaction

- **UI-11** Player actions must provide appropriate feedback.
    
- **UI-12** Actions with significant consequences must communicate their expected effects before execution where possible.
    
- **UI-13** Invalid or unavailable actions must explain why they cannot be performed.
    
- **UI-14** Long-running or time-consuming actions must communicate their duration or time cost.
    
- **UI-15** The interface must clearly communicate changes to resources, inventory, item condition and world state.
    

#### Visual Consistency

- **UI-16** The interface must maintain consistent visual conventions across all systems.
    
- **UI-17** Similar information must use consistent terminology, formatting and visual representation.
    
- **UI-18** Important states, such as damaged, unavailable, completed or newly discovered, must be visually distinguishable.
    
- **UI-19** The visual presentation should support the grounded medieval fantasy setting.
    

#### Player Experience

- **UI-20** The player must be able to understand the primary gameplay loop without external instructions.
    
- **UI-21** The player must be able to identify available actions and their requirements.
    
- **UI-22** The interface must avoid overwhelming the player with unnecessary simulation details.
    
- **UI-23** The player must be able to discover relevant information progressively as it becomes available.
    

### Extended requirements

- **UI-24** The interface may provide contextual information through tooltips or expandable details.
    
- **UI-25** The player may be able to customize certain interface elements.
    
- **UI-26** The interface may include visual representations of equipment, customers and historical events.
